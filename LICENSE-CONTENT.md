## A note on living traditions

Several traditions in this atlas — including Māori taniwha, Aboriginal
Australian Rainbow Serpent beings, and Indigenous North American horned
serpents — are living traditions that belong to their communities. The entries
aim to describe them respectfully, with named, verifiable sources (favouring
community-authored accounts where available). This license covers this
project's descriptive text, not the traditions themselves. If you reuse the
content, please carry that respect forward — and if you are a community member
and something here should be corrected, please open an issue.
